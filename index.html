Charge! Convergence

Three electron beams, red, green and blue, leave the gun and have to meet at one point on the screen. Stray charges bend each beam by a different amount. Place magnetic convergence strips until the test dots at the right turn pure white.

Mouse: click inside the tube to place a strip, click a strip to remove it, right click or R to rotate. Keys: arrows move the cursor, Space places, R rotate, P polarity, T dipole or quadrupole, 1 2 3 strength, U undo, C clear, A auto-place, N next level, F filter.

Reading the screen. The right column is the test pattern: five dots, one for each place on the screen the beams are aimed at, magnified 3x vertically. A white dot is converged, colour fringes show which beam is off and by how much (red high, blue low). CONV is the spread between the three beams in pixels; it must be 2 or less at every dot. PURITY is how far the three together miss the target, 6 or less. A dipole strip moves the beams together, a quadrupole strip pulls the outer two toward or away from the middle one, which is how convergence magnets work.

Descended from Charge! by C. Anthony Ray (BYTE, December 1982): the charges and their placement follow his listing. Other versions: Charge! Phosphor, the physics lab, the 1982 Applesoft listing.
