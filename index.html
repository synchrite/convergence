Charge! Phosphor

You are inside a cathode ray tube. The gun at the left fires 16 electrons. Charges in the vacuum bend each one (orange + pulls, blue - pushes). Land them evenly across the 16 bands of the phosphor on the right wall: one in every band. Two in a band over-drives it and leaves a burn.

(BYTE, December 1982, lines 1010 to 1090): the launch vector is scaled to speed 10, and at every step the charges add CG × q × d / |d|² (a 1/r pull or push, CG = 25) to it, with the same charge placement as lines 2070 to 2150.
