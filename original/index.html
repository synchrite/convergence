Charge!

Charge! was written by C. Anthony Ray of Urbana, Illinois in 1982 and published in BYTE magazine, December 1982, pages 112 to 120, as the second-place winner of the BYTE Game Contest. The program below is his original Applesoft BASIC listing, copyright 1982 C. Anthony Ray. It runs unchanged on an emulated Apple II (Applesoft interpreter, hi-res screen, speaker, and his own 6502 fanfare routine).

Type on the keyboard. Prompts follow the original: skill level (1 to 5 ions), number of players (1 to 4), names, then each turn enter the point you want to hit as x,y (for example 200,120). The lower right corner is 279,159.

Original code and game design: C. Anthony Ray, 1982. Source: BYTE, December 1982, © BYTE Publications Inc. This page is only an emulator and a transcription of the printed listing, made for preservation. It is not affiliated with the author or with BYTE.
